## 🕹️ About

Nivalis Nights is a cyberpunk slice-of-life simulation game with adventure and RPG elements, set in the towering city of Nivalis. Start with a humble noodle stand and build a nightlife empire through restaurants, bars, and clubs while forming relationships, customizing homes, fishing, and exploring the city. Developed by ION LANDS and published by 505 Games, the single-player game is planned for Windows PC release on September 29, 2026.

Nivalis Nights is for players who enjoy atmospheric cyberpunk worlds, relaxed life simulation, business management, exploration, and character-driven stories.

## ✨ Features

- 🍜 **Build Your Business** — Grow from a small noodle stand into successful restaurants and nightclubs.
- 🌆 **Explore Nivalis** — Discover a vast cyberpunk city stretching from the ocean to the clouds.
- 🤝 **Build Relationships** — Meet diverse residents, form friendships, create rivalries, and find romance.
- 🏠 **Make a Home** — Buy and decorate apartments and customize living spaces for yourself and friends.
- 🎣 **Fish the Canals** — Take your boat onto the water and fish beneath Nivalis's neon skyline.
- 🌦️ **Live by the Weather** — Experience changing days, nights, weather, curfews, and riskier after-dark choices.
- ♟️ **Play Mini-Games** — Challenge city residents to games and compete in a cyberpunk chess tournament.
